Coverage

ss is a snapshot every 10 minutes, so a connection that opens and closes between polls is missed, and bytes are that socket's lifetime at the moment it was sampled — a real download shows up big, but short transfers can be undercounted. Without root on a host, only warden's own user's sockets are attributed — never guessed.
